ABOUT ITI

Empowering learners through accessible education

Intellectual Training Institute (ITI) provides flexible online learning opportunities through short professional courses. Our approach is focused on accessible learning, useful skills and continuous personal development.

Whether you are developing your career, improving your professional skills or gaining knowledge in a new field, ITI provides a flexible learning environment that fits around your schedule.

Our focus

  • Accessible online education
  • Practical professional skills
  • Flexible self-paced learning
  • Continuous personal development
  • Assessment and certification

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How are ITI courses delivered?

ITI courses on this website are presented as online learning programmes. Specific delivery arrangements can be provided to registered learners.

Can I study at my own time?

Yes. ITI's online-learning model is designed to give learners flexibility to study around their schedules.

How do I register?

Use the registration form on this website. Your details can be sent to ITI through WhatsApp for follow-up.

Will I receive a certificate?

Certification is subject to the requirements of the selected course and ITI's applicable certification process.
